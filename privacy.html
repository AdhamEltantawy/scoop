Scoop privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Without an account

Scoop keeps your explored hexes, walks and settings on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere except map tile requests to the map provider you choose (OpenStreetMap or Esri).

With a Community account

Signing in is optional. If you do, Scoop stores this in its Supabase database:

  • Your email address (for sign-in codes) and the username, display name and home city you choose.
  • The ids of the street-sized hexagons (about 0.015 km² each) you walked through, when you first and last visited each one, how many walks went through it, and the country, city and district it's in. Visit times are only used to work out scores (hexes cool down over time); nobody can read them back, including you.
  • A daily total of the points you earned in each area, for weekly and monthly leaderboards.
  • Walk totals: start and end time, distance and the number of new hexes. Your GPS tracks are never uploaded.
  • Check-ins you make (the hex, the kind, an optional note and an optional photo, re-encoded so it carries no location data), kudos, friend requests, challenges and badges.

Who can see it

  • Your friends can see your profile, which hexes you explored and how often (not when), your walks, check-ins, badges, scores and home district. Friendships last a month unless you both renew.
  • Everyone else only sees an anonymous row with your score and rank on the leaderboards of the district, city and country you explored in.
  • Hexes inside your privacy zones (set in the menu, stored only on your device) are never uploaded, and are removed from the server if you add a zone later.

Looking up areas

When you're signed in, Scoop finds out which official district, city and country your hexes are in by asking the public Overpass API (OpenStreetMap data) about a point in a hex, rounded to about 10 m, then downloading that district's outline once. Further hexes inside it are matched on your device. Hexes inside privacy zones are never looked up.

Deleting your data

Community → Me → Delete my account permanently removes your account, photos and everything listed above from the server. Your map on the device is not affected; use Clear everything in the menu for that.
