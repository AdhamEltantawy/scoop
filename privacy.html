Scoop privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Without an account

Scoop keeps your explored hexes, walks and settings on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere except map tile requests to the map provider you choose (OpenStreetMap or Esri).

Walk reminders (Android app)

If you turn on Remind me to start a walk, Scoop asks Android's activity detection (Google Play services) to tell it when you start or stop walking, so it can offer to start a walk. It uses motion sensors, not GPS. Scoop only keeps when you last started walking and when it last reminded you, on your device, and never uploads any of it. Turn it off in Settings at any time.

With a Community account

Signing in is optional. If you do, Scoop stores this in its Supabase database:

  • Your email address (for sign-in codes) and the username, display name and home city you choose.
  • The ids of the street-sized hexagons (about 0.015 km² each) you walked through, when you first and last visited each one, how many walks went through it, and the country, city and district it's in. Visit times are used to work out scores (hexes cool down over time) and to bring your map to your other devices; only you can read them back, and only from your own account.
  • A daily total of the points you earned in each area, for weekly and monthly leaderboards.
  • Walk totals: start and end time, distance and the number of new hexes. Your GPS tracks are never uploaded.
  • Check-ins you make (the hex, the kind, an optional note and an optional photo, re-encoded so it carries no location data), kudos, friend requests, challenges and badges.
  • Landmarks you leave (the hex, the kind, a title and an optional note), and which landmarks you found and when. Finding one only tells the server you passed that hex; nobody else sees who found a landmark, the author only sees how many explorers did.

Who can see it

  • Your friends can see your profile, which hexes you explored and how often (not when), your walks, check-ins, badges, scores and home district. Friendships last a month unless you both renew.
  • Every signed-in explorer can see your landmarks (where they are, the title and note), but only your friends see that you left them; to everyone else they're from an anonymous explorer.
  • Everyone else only sees an anonymous row with your score and rank on the leaderboards of the district, city and country you explored in, and on the leaderboard of everyone.
  • Hexes inside your privacy zones (set in the menu, stored only on your device) are never uploaded, and are removed from the server if you add a zone later, along with any check-ins and landmarks there.

Looking up areas

When you're signed in, Scoop finds out which official district, city and country your hexes are in by asking the public Overpass API (OpenStreetMap data) about a point in a hex, rounded to about 10 m, then downloading that district's outline once. Further hexes inside it are matched on your device. Hexes inside privacy zones are never looked up.

Deleting your data

Community → Me → Delete my account permanently removes your account, photos and everything listed above from the server. Your map on the device is not affected; use Clear everything in the menu for that.
