Scoop privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Everything stays on your phone

Scoop has no accounts and no servers. Your explored hexes, walks, check-ins, photos, settings and the Scoop files friends send you are stored only on your device. The only network requests are map tiles from the map provider you choose (OpenStreetMap or Esri).

What you choose to share

  • Scoop file (Community → Share my Scoop file): your name, the ids of the street-sized hexagons (about 0.015 km² each) you walked through with the day you first visited each and how often, walk totals (start and end time, distance, new hexes), check-ins (place, kind, time and note — not photos) and challenges. It never contains GPS tracks.
  • Share card: an image of the hexes on screen and your totals.
  • Backup: your map, walks, check-ins (not photos), name and challenges, for your own safekeeping.

These go only where you send them — Scoop never sends them anywhere by itself. Anyone you send a Scoop file to can see where you've walked, so share it with people you trust.

Privacy zones

Hexes and check-ins inside your privacy zones (set in the menu) are left out of Scoop files and share cards.

Deleting data

Clear everything in the menu erases your map and check-ins. Remove a friend under Community → Friends to delete their file from your phone. Uninstalling the app (or clearing site data on the web) removes everything.
